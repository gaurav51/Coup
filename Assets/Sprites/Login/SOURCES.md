# Login icon sources

- GoogleG.png: official Google identity G icon, downloaded unchanged from
  https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png
  Reference: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines
- GuestGold_v1.png: generated specifically for Coup with transparent background.

Labels are rendered by TextMeshPro, not baked into the images.
